Premier League Predictor

A data-only model — recent form, home/away record, goal & xG output, opponent-adjusted strength and head-to-head. No bookmaker odds. Values are win/draw/win probabilities, not certainties.

How to read it. Each bar splits a match into home win / draw / away win. The pick is simply the most likely single outcome — a 45% pick is a lean, not a lock.
  • Honest accuracy: ~47% across a 1,900-match backtest (vs 44% for always picking home). Football is high-variance by nature.
  • Possession (season average) is shown per match as context only. It was tested as a model feature and left out — it didn't improve accuracy, as ball control is already captured by the xG-strength rating.
  • Not included: injuries and confirmed line-ups (the latter aren't published until ~1h before kickoff).
